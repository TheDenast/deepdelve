# Alpha 0.3.0

Creative mode: walk the caverns on foot, and build in them, as in Minecraft's creative mode.

## Building

- Place blocks with the right mouse button, against the face you're looking at
- A hotbar of nine blocks, chosen with 1-9 or the mouse wheel, drawn as little cubes
- Middle click picks the block you're looking at into the hotbar
- A creative inventory on E, after early Minecraft: every block in a scrolling window, to drag or
  click onto the hotbar. Shift-click adds a block to the first empty slot, and right-click
  empties a slot

## Moving

- Collision, walking, sprinting and jumping, with Minecraft's sizes and speeds
- Sneaking stops you walking off edges
- Swimming in water and lava, and climbing out onto the shore
- Press Space twice to fly, and land to stop
- N switches to the old free flight through everything, and back
- You now start on the ground

## Known limitations

- Brightness has only been tuned from screenshots, and may look different on a real display
- Small conifers look a bit like cacti up close
- The world's underside is very dim when seen from the void below
- No creatures, health or fall damage, and lava is harmless
- Water and lava can't be placed, and crystal you place doesn't light its surroundings
- Edits, placed blocks included, only show within about 384 blocks, and aren't saved to disk
