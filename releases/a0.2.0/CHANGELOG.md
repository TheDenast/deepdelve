# Alpha 0.2.0

The world gets its character: three distinct layers to explore, with calmer ground and textures
that no longer show an obvious repeat.

## World

- Three themed layers replace the random tiers: Overworld, Dark Forest and Underworld
- The Overworld has blue crystal skies, lakes and seas, and cozy green trees
- The Dark Forest has very dense conifers, and bare mountains rising out of them
- The Underworld has lava lakes, black rock mountains with glowing veins, and no plants
- Bedrock caps both ends of the world, with empty void beyond
- Level plains between the hills of the Overworld and Dark Forest

## Graphics

- Lava, as a second liquid that lights the Underworld from below
- Every texture is now painted by code
- Each block's texture gets a random orientation, and ground textures are evened out, which hides
  repeats across large surfaces

## Known limitations

- Brightness has only been tuned from screenshots, and may look different on a real display
- Small conifers look a bit like cacti up close
- The world's underside is very dim when seen from the void below
- Still no collision, block placing, inventory or creatures
- Edits only show within about 384 blocks, and aren't saved to disk
